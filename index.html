ABOUT ROBO TITANS

More Than a
Competition

Robo Titans #123 is a national-level robotics competition for undergraduate and postgraduate students. It's a platform to learn, innovate, and showcase real-world problem-solving through robotics.

Led by Captain & Driver Rayan Dobani alongside Vice Captain & Human Player Aakhya Patki, our bot TITAN-X123 features a high-RPM vertical kinetic drum engineered by Master Builder Krinza Mardani, hardware telemetry from Technical Head Bhuvi Dubey and Tech Specialists Aradhya Dandey & Yati Agrawal, predictive scouting by Gaurav Chauhan, and live media outreach by Dhruvi Pote & Shreesha.
